Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site must do. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us about your business, and we'll give you a clear quote.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website we build is designed for phones, tablets and computers from the start.

Can you add forms, payments or logins?

Yes. If your business needs a contact form, online payments or customer logins, tell us and we'll advise on the best way to do it safely.

Can you update my website later?

Yes. Our maintenance service covers updates, fixes and regular check-ups.

Do I need a privacy policy?

If your website collects any personal information, South Africa's POPIA law says you should have one. We include a privacy policy and terms page on every site.
